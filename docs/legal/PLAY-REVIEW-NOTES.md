**What the app is.** Crichere is software for amateur, real-world cricket leagues in India. Organizers
create a league, players and franchise owners join it, and the organizer runs a live player auction to
form the teams. The cricket itself is played in person, on real grounds.

**No betting or gambling.** The app has no betting, wagering, lotteries, fantasy contests or games of
chance. Users cannot stake anything on a match or auction result. Our Terms prohibit using the service
for betting or gambling.

**The auction is virtual.** Each franchise gets a virtual budget, used only to pick its squad. Amounts
are shown in rupees, but bids and sale prices are not payments. Nothing is charged, paid or transferred
because of a bid or sale, inside or outside the app, and the amounts have no cash value.

**No payments in the app.** Crichere does not process, hold or transfer money and does not use Google
Play Billing. A league organizer can set a joining fee for taking part in their real-world league.
That fee is paid directly to the organizer outside the app. Users can upload a screenshot as proof for
the organizer. Crichere takes no share of it.

**League awards.** Organizers can list awards for their real-world league. These are decided and given
by the organizer, not by Crichere, and are not won through anything in the app.

**User content.** Open any league and tap the ⋮ on a player or franchise row to report or block that
person; "Report league" and "Block organizer" are at the end of the league page. Blocked people are listed under My Profile >
Blocked people. Our team reviews reports every day and can remove content or suspend accounts. Users
must tick their acceptance of the Terms and Privacy Policy before they can sign in. The app is for
users aged 18 and over.

**How to sign in.** Enter the phone number from the App access instructions (10 digits, without +91),
tick the Terms box and tap Send code. Enter the fixed code from the same instructions. The account
already has a profile.

**Test data.** Open the league "<sample league name>" from the dashboard. It has players, franchises
and a completed auction, so you can see rosters, results and the ⋮ menu on each row.
