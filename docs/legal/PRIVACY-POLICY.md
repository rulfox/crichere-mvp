## 1. Data we collect

**You give us**

| Data | Why | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile number (India, +91) | Sign-in by one-time password (OTP); identifies your account | Yes |
| Name | Shown to organisers and other participants | Yes |
| Profile photo | Shown on your profile, in league rosters and in the live auction | Yes |
| Home state and district | Show nearby leagues; shown on your profile | Yes |
| Playing role, batting hand, bowling style | Player cards and auction listings | Yes (bowling style only for bowlers and all-rounders) |
| League content you create: league name, description, logo, banner, ground, rules, fees, dates, auction settings | Run and display your league | Only if you organise a league |
| Ground details you register: name and map coordinates | Let leagues pick a ground | Only if you register a ground |
| Payment proof screenshots (for example a UPI receipt) | Let a league organiser confirm that you paid a league fee | Only if you join a paid league |
| Bids, franchise claims, join requests, follows | Operate the auction and leagues | Created by your actions |

**Collected automatically or from your device**

- **Approximate location (Android: `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`).** Only when you tap "Use my location" or "Show nearest to me". Used to fill your state and district, or to sort leagues by distance. We do not track your location in the background.
- **Push notification token** (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send auction and league alerts. You can switch notifications off in your device settings.
- **Technical data** such as IP address, device and app version, and request logs, kept for security, abuse prevention and debugging.
- **Search text** you type in the ground search is sent to Google Places to return suggestions (see section 3).

**We do not collect** your contacts, SMS inbox, call log, microphone recordings, precise background location, government ID, or bank / card / UPI credentials. Crichere does not process payments: league fees are paid directly between you and the organiser outside the app, and you upload a screenshot as proof. Cover or crop anything in the screenshot you do not want the organiser to see.

## 2. How we use it

- Create and secure your account and sign you in.
- Show your profile and player card to the organisers and participants of leagues you join or view.
- Run leagues, the live auction, rosters, results and notifications.
- Let organisers verify league-fee payments.
- Show leagues near you.
- Prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms, comply with law, and fix bugs.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising. We do not run advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

## 3. Who sees your data

**Other users.** Your name, photo, state, district, playing details and league activity (including auction bids and results) are visible to organisers and participants of the leagues you join. Public league pages and shared links (crichere.com) can show a league's name, ground, district, state and live auction results to anyone with the link.

**Payment screenshots** are visible only to you and the organiser of that league. They are stored privately and served through short-lived links.

**Service providers (processors)** that handle data on our behalf:

| Provider | Purpose | Data |
|---|---|---|
| MSG91 | Sends sign-in OTP SMS | Mobile number |
| Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging) | Push notifications | Notification token |
| Google Maps and Places | Map display and ground search | Search text, map coordinates, device and app identifiers as per Google's policy |
| Amazon Web Services (S3) | Stores photos, logos, banners and payment screenshots | Uploaded images |
| Railway | Hosts our servers and database | All account and league data |

These providers may process data outside India. We share only what each needs.

**Legal and safety.** We disclose data if required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users or Crichere.

**Business transfer.** If Crichere is merged or acquired, data may transfer to the successor, bound by this policy.

## 5. Your rights and choices

Under the DPDP Act you may:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you (most of it is visible in My Profile).
- **Correct or update** it (Edit profile in the app).
- **Delete** your account and data. Email hello@crichere.com from the address you registered, or write with your registered mobile number and the subject "Delete my account". We act within 30 days. [An in-app "Delete account" option and a web deletion page are planned; update this line when live.]
- **Withdraw consent** (for example turn off location or notifications in device settings, or delete your account). Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing.
- **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights if you die or cannot act.
- **Complain** to our Grievance Officer (above). We reply within [7] days and resolve within 30 days. If unsatisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

## 6. Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in uses OTP and short-lived tokens. Payment screenshots are private. Access to servers and storage is restricted. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting you occurs we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 7. Children

Crichere is not for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.
